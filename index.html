The Operating Model · Four Stages

Connect. Measure. Manage. Transform.

Univers creates a self-reinforcing operational flywheel. Every device connected compounds the intelligence of the entire system, driving continuous optimization from day one.

01 / CONNECT

Connect

Ingest physical reality. Connect on-the-ground operational technology with cloud intelligence across energy, buildings, ports, and manufacturing without hardware lock-in.

02 / MEASURE

Measure

Convert physics to verified data. 450M+ connected sensors & devices are fused into accurate, audit-ready energy baselines and real-time carbon telemetry.

03 / MANAGE

Manage

Move from awareness to control. Real-time telemetry is translated into automated setpoint tuning, closed-loop dispatch, and demand-response peak shedding.

04 / TRANSFORM

Transform

Compound intelligence network-wide. Optimization returns are reinvested into systemic decarbonization across 1,070 GW of managed global energy assets.
