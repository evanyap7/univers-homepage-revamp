The Fragmentation Trap

Awareness without a route to abatement. Measurement without meaning.

  • Disconnected Data: Energy, carbon, and asset data trapped in separate platforms and spreadsheets across teams and geographies.
  • Manual Reporting: Weeks spent reconciling Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions by hand before a single decision can be made.
  • Siloed Stakeholders: Facilities, sustainability, and finance teams working from different numbers, different tools, different truths.
  • No Path to Action: Data that describes the problem but never compounds into a plan for solving it.
